Cheat sheet: reading y = a(x − h)² + k
(h, k)
The vertex. h is the number inside the parentheses with its sign flipped. k is the number at the end, sign kept.
a
If a is negative, the parabola opens down. It is reflected over the x-axis. A positive a opens up.
x − h
(x − 3)² moves the graph right 3. (x + 3)² moves it left 3.
+ k
+ 5 moves the graph up 5. − 5 moves it down 5.
